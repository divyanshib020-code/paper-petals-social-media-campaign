# Paper Petals — Sample Social Media Campaign

## About the Project

Paper Petals is a fictional stationery brand created as part of a self-initiated social media marketing portfolio project.

The project explores how to plan a small Instagram campaign using a consistent pastel visual style, simple Canva designs, captions, calls to action, and a content calendar.

## Campaign Objectives

* Introduce the fictional stationery brand.
* Showcase stationery through visually consistent social media posts.
* Encourage audience interaction through comments and saves.
* Plan content and identify engagement metrics to track.

## Target Audience

Students and stationery enthusiasts interested in study desk organisation, planners, and aesthetically pleasing stationery.

## Content Plan

The campaign includes three planned static Instagram posts:

1. **Meet Paper Petals** — brand introduction.
2. **Your Study Desk Essentials** — stationery showcase and audience interaction.
3. **Plan Your Week With Me** — weekly planning and save-focused content.

## Tools Used

* Canva — social media post designs
* Google Sheets — content calendar
* Google Docs — campaign documentation

## Project Contents

* `Canva_Posts/` — final post designs
* `Paper_Petals_Campaign.pdf` — campaign portfolio document
* `Content_Calendar.pdf` — content calendar
* `Paper_Petals_Social_Media_Captions.docx` — caption document

## Learning Outcomes

Through this project, I practised basic social media content planning, Canva-based design, caption writing, calls to action, content calendar organisation, and identifying engagement metrics.

## Project Status

This is a self-initiated sample project for learning and portfolio purposes. Paper Petals is a fictional brand, and the posts have not been published as a live campaign. No actual campaign performance results are claimed.
