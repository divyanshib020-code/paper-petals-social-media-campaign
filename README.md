# Paper Petals — Sample Social Media Campaign

## About the Project

Paper Petals is a fictional stationery brand created as part of a self-initiated social media marketing portfolio project.

This project explores how to plan a small Instagram campaign using a consistent pastel visual style, simple Canva designs, captions, calls to action, and a content calendar.

## Campaign Objectives

* Introduce the fictional stationery brand.
* Showcase stationery products through visually consistent social media posts.
* Encourage audience interaction through comments and saves.
* Organise the posting schedule and identify engagement metrics to track.

## Target Audience

Students and stationery enthusiasts interested in study desk organisation, planners, and aesthetically pleasing stationery.

## Content Plan

The campaign includes three Instagram static posts:

1. **Meet Paper Petals** — Introduces the brand and its visual identity.
2. **Your Study Desk Essentials** — Showcases stationery and encourages audience interaction.
3. **Plan Your Week With Me** — Promotes weekly planning and encourages users to save the post.

## Tools Used

* **Canva** — Creating social media post designs using templates.
* **Google Sheets** — Planning and organising the content calendar.
* **Google Docs** — Documenting the campaign and preparing the portfolio.

## Engagement and Performance Tracking

The campaign identifies the following metrics for potential tracking:

* **Reach:** Number of unique accounts that see a post.
* **Likes and comments:** Indicators of audience interaction.
* **Profile visits:** An indication of interest in the brand.
* **Saves:** A useful metric for content that audiences may want to revisit.

## Learning Outcomes

Through this project, I practised:

* Planning a basic social media content calendar.
* Creating simple, template-based Canva graphics.
* Writing social media captions and calls to action.
* Organising campaign content and scheduling information.
* Identifying relevant engagement metrics.

## Project Status

This is a self-initiated portfolio project for a fictional stationery brand. The posts have not been published as a live campaign, and no actual engagement or performance results are claimed.
